Report2Alpha-Bench: Evaluating LLM Agents on End-to-End Quantitative Research

Yun Zhang, Hui Yang, Wei Meng · 2611.27635 · 2026-11-20 · Science & research
Planning / reasoningResearch agents

We evaluate whether LLM agents can independently carry out a several-hour quantitative research project: read a brokerage research report, make a plan, and over 7 rounds of iteration propose hypotheses, write factor code and improve it from backtest feedback, seeing only 2022-2023 market data. Like Terminal-Bench-Science, the task evaluates overall research ability; unlike it, there is no directly checkable unique answer: the model must repeatedly propose hypotheses on very noisy data, revise its understanding from experiments, disentangle multiple factors and design ablations without overfitting in-sample, much like complex real-world decisions with many interacting factors. After the research, a fixed rule using only 2022-2023 results selects a low-correlated factor pool from all candidates the model produced, and scores it on unseen 2024-2025 data after removing common style returns such as size and momentum; the same rule on round-0 candidates alone gives a report baseline that "implements the report without iterating". We run gpt-6.1-sol (reasoning effort xhigh), gpt-6.1-terra (xhigh), muse-spark-1.3 (xhigh) and deepseek-v4.1-flash (max) once on each of 7 reports. Of the 28 runs, 19 factor pools pass the usability threshold; the average score is 48.4 (0-100 scale), against 25.4 for the report baseline and 35.9 for the models' own picks. From similar report baselines (17.0-31.0), the models separate after iteration to 74.7 (gpt-6.1-sol), 56.6 (gpt-6.1-terra), 35.8 (deepseek-v4.1-flash) and 26.2 (muse-spark-1.3); their candidates look equally good in training and diverge only in testing, with the leaders proposing more diverse ideas per round and ending with more effective factors in the pool. Evaluating research agents thus requires scoring on frozen future data after removing styles, and measuring "what the research produced" separately from "what was finally picked".
